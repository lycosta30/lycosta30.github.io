Welcome Home!

I got an AIM message from the devil last night under the username [lov3r0fS1n666].

He told me to move to Iowa. I was hesitant at first. It had only been two months since I’d moved from parents’ house in New Jersey out to Colorado for various career-based reasons, none of which ever picked up, nor overshadowed the personal reasons, but mostly due to the fact that weed was legal and it was too tempting to spend the most of my days feeling like I was underwater, staring through old, melted glass from inside of a fish tank

I wasn’t convinced the devil knew all about the intricacies of being a human renter, being a human render at best. In fact, I questioned a lot about what he knew of humanity in recent years, seeing as how he was contacting me through an outdated medium and had an All American Rejects lyric as his profile description. Then again, as the first entity to contact me in a couple of months, I questioned what I myself knew of humanity, seeing as how I’d been scrolling through old AIM messages between me and some long-gone friend who could have been dead or maybe an adult when the devil’s name appeared in my inbox. He told me that this method of contact was to make him feel more tangible, to convince me it wasn’t just a dream, that he was truly the devil. I laughed and told him it wasn’t working. He retaliated by lighting my shirt on fire through the screen.
